# shimarisu-dakeapp-site

SHIMARISU brand site for the DAKE ecosystem.

## Position

- PEAKHEADZ is the umbrella.
- SHIMARISU is the practical judgment layer.
- DAKE is the practical tool group.
- SHIMARISU and DAKE are parallel roles, not a vertical chain.
- SHIMARISU judges the next action; DAKE processes the task.

## Site Role

- Keep the SHIMARISU positioning quiet and clear.
- Avoid turning the site into a DAKE app list.
- Avoid AI chatbot framing.
- Do not mix in unrelated real-estate site content.
